About alfanar Power

A New Light in Clean Power, Backed by Five Decades of Delivery

alfanar Power is the newest company of the alfanar Group, the Riyadh-based industrial group founded in 1976. Our name means "lighthouse" in Arabic, and our purpose follows from it: guide the energy transition with dependable, clean power.

We build on the renewables track record of alfanar Projects across Saudi Arabia, Egypt, India, Spain and the UK, covering solar, wind and green hydrogen, with 1.75 GW of renewable projects to date.
